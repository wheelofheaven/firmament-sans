# Firmament Greek — design brief

Greek is the first original extension planned for Firmament Sans: it shares the
Latin baseline and x-height model, Jost carries no Greek beyond four symbol-use
letters (Δ Ω μ π), and the corpus needs polytonic text (Theogony, Matthew,
Revelation, the Greek Enoch) as well as inline citations on every locale page.

This directory holds the brief, not outlines. The extension is drawn in editable
source masters, not generated by `scholar/scripts/build.py`.

## Repertoire

`repertoire.json` lists every Greek codepoint observed in the published corpus
(174 distinct, 283,346 occurrences; 64 in Greek and Coptic, 110 in Greek
Extended), with counts, canonical decompositions, and an example path each.
That is the **test floor**. The design target is:

- all Greek letters of U+0370–03FF (not the Coptic letters, not the archaic
  numerals unless a reader asks for them);
- the whole of Greek Extended U+1F00–1FFF, drawn as precomposed glyphs;
- combining breathings, accents, dialytika and ypogegrammeni (U+0300, 0301,
  0304, 0306, 0308, 0313, 0314, 0342, 0345) with mark-to-base and mark-to-mark
  positioning, so decomposed input shapes identically to precomposed input;
- Greek punctuation U+037E and U+0387, and the final sigma U+03C2 with a
  reviewed `calt`/contextual rule for automatic final-form substitution where
  editors want it (off by default; the corpus already encodes ς explicitly).

Mathematical Greek symbols are out of scope; they are not Greek text coverage.

## Metric contract

Measured from the Firmament 0.1 outlines and published with the companion
proof (<https://firmament.wheelofheaven.world/companions/>):

| Measure | Regular (400) | Bold (700) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Units per em | 1000 | 1000 |
| x-height / cap height | 460 / 700 | 460 / 700 |
| Ascender / descender | 1070 / −375 | 1070 / −375 |
| Stem (Latin l, mid-height) | 80 | 152 |
| Typographic colour (‰ of em², Gen 1:1 EN) | 184.6 | 283.9 |

Greek lowercase sits on the Latin x-height (ο at 460 plus overshoot), capitals
on the cap height, and the plain vertical of ι, τ and the stems of π at the
Latin stem. Descenders of β, γ, ζ, μ, ξ, ρ, φ, χ, ψ stay above −375.

## What the proof showed

Of the open candidates measured against the contract, none fits: GFS
Neohellenic (the 1927 New Hellenic, the closest design relative) runs at 0.65×
the Regular stem with an x-height of 394; Didact Gothic at 0.85× with no bold;
Noto Sans fits the stem (1.1×/0.98×) but its x-height of 546 reads a size
larger. That gap is the reason to draw Firmament Greek rather than pair.

## Review gates

- Shape `repertoire.json` through HarfBuzz in both NFC and NFD; zero `.notdef`.
- Proof the Theogony opening, Matthew 1, and Revelation 1 at 16, 19 and 24 px
  next to Firmament Latin, in all four styles.
- Stacked breathing + accent on capitals and lowercase without collision; iota
  subscript and adscript both reviewed.
- Native-reader review of paragraphs, not alphabet sheets, before release.
